## Why

In a shared checkout, workflow notes can be overwritten and folder-wide task discovery can route a session to another change.

## What Changes

- Scope intake, review, and continuation state to the session.
- Bind a session explicitly to its task document and reserve the corresponding work unit.
- Require ownership for Nereus archive and serialize updates to canonical OpenSpec specs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `session-documents`: workflow document isolation within one checkout.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

Nereus workflow skills, task discovery, HUD, and lifecycle hooks.
